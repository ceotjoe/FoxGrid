FoxGrid is a client for a self-hosted server, so there is no account or login. To review the app:
1. Open the app and enter the server address {{DEMO_SERVER}} in the "Server" field.
2. Enter any callsign (for example DL0TEST) and this invite token: {{DEMO_INVITE}}
3. Tap "Beitreten" (Join). The demo mission contains simulated bearings from five stations near Munich.
The app uses the location only while it is open (to attach your position to bearings). No background location, no tracking, no ads. The UI language is German.
