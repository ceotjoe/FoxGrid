FoxGrid helps a group of radio amateurs find an interferer on a 70 cm repeater together. Every participant records bearings, FoxGrid fuses them live into the most likely location of the interferer and shows it to everyone on the map right away.

IMPORTANT: FoxGrid is a client for your own FoxGrid server, which your group runs itself (open source, MIT license). The app cannot be used without a server and an invitation. The app's publisher receives no data.

RECORD BEARINGS
• Position, azimuth, uncertainty and field strength in a few taps
• Azimuth is always stored as true north; magnetic input is converted with the declination at your position
• Uncertainty is mandatory: sharp ±5°, medium ±15°, poor ±30°
• Bearings are never deleted, only flagged as outliers or deactivated

LIVE ESTIMATE
• All bearings are fused into one most likely location
• Error ellipse (1σ/2σ) and probability areas (50/80/95 %) on the map
• Single outliers barely move the result
• Filter by transmission and time

HOMING
• Arrow and distance to the estimated source
• Signal trend with S-meter input (IARU convention) and attenuator setting
• Signal levels refine the estimate further

NEXT BEARING
• The app suggests positions where one more bearing would shrink the error ellipse the most

TEAMWORK
• Chat for the whole operation or per team
• Tasks with assignee and map position
• Fixed bearing stations can feed bearings automatically via MQTT

WORKS OFFLINE
• Everything you enter is stored locally first and synced automatically later
• Sync status is always visible

PRIVACY
• Sign in with an invite link and your callsign: no passwords, no account
• Location is used only while the app is open, for bearings and the team map
• Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap
