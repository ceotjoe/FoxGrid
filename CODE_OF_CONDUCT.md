# Code of conduct

FoxGrid is a project by and for radio amateurs. We treat each other respectfully, factually and helpfully,
just as you would expect on a good repeater.

- Criticism of code and ideas is welcome, personal attacks are not.
- No discrimination, harassment or insults, in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Do not publish other people's private data (locations, callsign assignments, contact details) without their consent.
- FoxGrid exists to find interference. Calls for harassment, jamming or vigilantism against identified people are
  not welcome here; such contributions will be removed.

Violations can be reported privately to the maintainer (GitHub: [@ceotjoe](https://github.com/ceotjoe)).
The maintainer may remove contributions and exclude people from participating.
